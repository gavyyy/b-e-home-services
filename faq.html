Helpful answers

Questions? We’ve got you.

How do I get a quote?

Use the free quote form to tell us about the job, choose your preferred date, and add photos if helpful. The calculator shows a starting estimate; B & E confirms final pricing before the work is booked.

Is my requested date automatically booked?

No. You can request a preferred service date and arrival window, but B & E will confirm availability with you before the job is scheduled.

What services do you offer?

We provide lawn care and landscaping, hedge trimming, tree trimming, mulching, pressure washing, residential cleaning, commercial cleaning, deep cleaning, and basic home cleaning.

What should I do before the team arrives?

Please secure valuables, pets, fragile items, and vehicles; share any access details; and let us know about existing damage, hazards, hidden conditions, irrigation, or special-care instructions before work begins.

How do I check my quote or schedule?

Use the Customer Access section on the home page with the same email address you used for your quote request.

What if I have a concern after service?

Please contact B & E as soon as possible. We will review the concern and make reasonable efforts to address it. See our Terms of Use for property-condition and damage details.
