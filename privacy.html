Privacy Policy

B & E Home Services collects the details you provide when requesting a quote, including your name, contact information, service request, preferred scheduling details, and optional job photos.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to quote requests, plan or schedule services, send confirmations, and improve customer service. We do not sell personal information.

Quote accounts

If you create a customer quote account, your email address is used to securely show only the quote requests associated with that email. Keep your password private.

Photos and communications

Optional job photos are used to understand your request. Email confirmations and quote-related communications are sent using the contact information you provide.
